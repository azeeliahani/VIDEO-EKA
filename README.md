# EKA explainers

## Why many knowledge hubs fail: 7 traps and how to dodge them (60 s)

**Video:** `output/v4/eka_why_knowledge_hubs_fail_60s.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, voice-over, music and sound effects)

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0–7.5 s | "Uh-oh!" EKA drops in: *Lessons learned: why many knowledge hubs fail*. Seven warning markers circle EKA |
| 7.5–42.5 s | 7 traps × 5 s. Each one shows a jittery trap icon, **What actually happens**, then **How to dodge it** with a "DODGED!" stamp. The fixed icon then joins EKA's orbit |
| 42.5–52.5 s | "How to dodge every trap": a checklist that ticks off as EKA names each fix |
| 52.5–60 s | "Dodge the traps, and your hub becomes the place everyone trusts!" |

| Trap | What actually happens | How to dodge it |
|---|---|---|
| Technology-first thinking | The agent launches before the content is ready, so early answers disappoint | Get the content right first, then launch the agent |
| No content ownership | Documents get old, and nobody is accountable for updating them | Give every piece of content a named owner |
| Garbage in, garbage out | Duplicates and old versions compete, so answers conflict | Merge duplicates and keep one source of truth |
| Metadata as an afterthought | Optional, inconsistent tags make content hard to filter or trust | Make key tags required, and keep them consistent |
| Governance only on paper | Rules exist, but depend on people remembering to follow them | Build the rules into automated workflows |
| No feedback loop | Nobody tracks unanswered questions, so the gaps stay open | Track feedback and close the gaps, regularly |
| Weak change management | People keep asking colleagues, because the hub's value was never shown | Show quick wins, train people, and celebrate the hub |

- **Narration:** `assets/voiceover/eka_vo_v4.wav`, generated by `scripts/make_voiceover_v4.py`. The lines are in that script.
- **Animation:** `src/eka_v4.html`, with timings in `src/timeline_v4.js`.
- **Audio:** `scripts/make_audio_v4.py` makes the music, the sound effects and the mix. The separate tracks are in `output/v4/`.
- **Rebuild:** `python3 scripts/make_audio_v4.py --vo assets/voiceover/eka_vo_v4.wav && MIX=mix_vo.wav VIDEO=v4 node scripts/render.js`

## What it takes to build a Knowledge Hub with an AI Agent (60 s)

**Video:** `output/v3/eka_knowledge_hub_ai_agent_60s.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, voice-over, music and sound effects)

EKA presents the eight essentials in dark blue, white and green.
EKA drops in, blinks, hops, points and lip-syncs to the voice.
As each essential is explained, its icon flies onto an orbit around EKA.

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0–7.5 s | "What does it take? To build a Knowledge Hub with an AI Agent." The orbit of eight appears around EKA |
| 7.5–47.5 s | 8 steps × 5 s: Understand, Content, Metadata, Format, Governance, Automation, AI Agent ("That's me!"), Analytics. Each step has an animated icon, *what to do* and **Why it matters** |
| 47.5–52.5 s | The full orbit gathers around EKA: trusted content, clear governance, continuous improvement |
| 52.5–60 s | "When these foundations are in place, your AI agent delivers accurate knowledge & helps people work smarter!" |

**Narration** (`assets/voiceover/eka_vo_v3.wav`): an excited, bubbly voice modelled on the ElevenLabs Allison reference (`assets/source/voice_reference_allison_energetic.mp3`).
It is generated offline by `scripts/make_voiceover_v3.py` (Kokoro TTS, a blend of three expressive voices).
The lines are in that script, and each one fits its scene.

**Files**
- `src/eka_v3.html` is the animation, and `src/timeline_v3.js` holds the scene and voice cue timings.
- `scripts/make_audio_v3.py` makes the music, the sound effects and the final mix (`output/v3/mix_vo.wav`). The music is ducked under the voice.
- `output/v3/voice.wav`, `music.wav` and `sfx.wav` are the separate stems.

**Rebuild**

```bash
npm install
python3 scripts/make_voiceover_v3.py          # optional: regenerate the voice (setup steps are in the script)
python3 scripts/make_audio_v3.py --vo assets/voiceover/eka_vo_v3.wav
MIX=mix_vo.wav VIDEO=v3 node scripts/render.js
```

To use an ElevenLabs recording instead, pass its MP3 to `--vo`, adjust the cue times in `src/timeline_v3.js` (`me`, `found`), and re-render.

## v1: Building an AI-Powered Knowledge Hub

A 60-second motion graphic explainer for beginners, presented by **EKA** the robot.
It turns the *"Eight building blocks of an AI-powered knowledge hub"* slide into an animated story.

**Final video:** `output/eka_knowledge_hub_60s.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, with music and sound effects)

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0–5 s | EKA drops in, lands with a bounce and waves: "Hi, I'm EKA!" The title builds and the 8 blocks bounce in |
| 5–10 s | The problem: messy, duplicated files and confused question marks. The files get sucked away and turn into 8 building blocks |
| 10–50 s | One card per block, 5 s each, each with its own animated icon. A progress tracker at the bottom ticks off each block as EKA presents it |
| 40–45 s | Block 07, Agent: EKA's moment. EKA grows and glows, sparkles appear, and a live chat demo plays |
| 50–55 s | Recap: the blocks stack into a house with an "AI Knowledge Hub" roof, then confetti |
| 55–60 s | Call to action: *Start with Block 01: Understand* |

## Audio

The music and sound effects are synthesised from scratch in `scripts/make_audio.py`, so they are royalty-free.
The music is an upbeat 96 BPM marimba, bell and drum groove, and the scenes change on its bar lines.
There are about 150 sound-effect cues: pops, whooshes, boings, robot beeps, dings, clanks, zaps, sparkles and a ta-da.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `output/mix.wav` | Music and SFX together (used in the final video) |
| `output/music.wav` | Music bed only |
| `output/sfx.wav` | Sound effects only |
| `output/eka_knowledge_hub_60s_noaudio.mp4` | Picture only, for editing in another tool |

To add the voice-over, see [VOICEOVER_SCRIPT.md](VOICEOVER_SCRIPT.md).
It has a line-by-line script timed to each scene, and `scripts/add_voiceover.sh` mixes in your recording and ducks the music under it automatically.

## Rebuilding

Requires Node 18+, Python 3 with numpy and Pillow, ffmpeg, and Playwright's Chromium.

```bash
npm install
npm run cutout     # cut EKA out of the white background and split off the waving arm
npm run audio      # synthesise music.wav, sfx.wav and mix.wav
npm run frames     # render 1800 frames with Playwright and encode them with ffmpeg
```

To preview in a browser, open `src/video.html#play`.
For quick stills, run `node scripts/render.js --stills 12.5,42`.
The text, colours and timings are all in `src/video.html`. The `BLOCKS` array holds each card's wording.
